Property Description

Berkeley Residency is a twin-tower residential development rising 18 floors above one of Nairobi's most established green corridors, where the Westlands business district meets the tree-canopied calm of Kileleshwa. Built across two towers — Block A and Block B — the development brings together 120 homes designed for people who want city convenience without giving up quiet, leafy surroundings. Rather than the usual courtyard-facing apartment block, Berkeley is built around vertical living: every home looks out over the Nairobi skyline, arboretum canopy, or the surrounding residential avenues, with a full rooftop level dedicated to recreation, wellness, and entertaining above the noise of the street. luxury.

Property Features

  • The Rooftop Amenity Deck

    What sets Berkeley apart is its elevated communal level — a full rooftop precinct rather than a single amenity or two. Residents step out of the lift into the following. An open-air rooftop pool deck with panoramic city views. A climate-controlled indoor kids' play zone, shielded from weather and street noise. A rooftop gym and wellness deck. A resident lounge and terrace for informal gatherings. On-site café/restaurant and retail arcade. A patio gaming zone for evening downtime • Landscaped courtyard and terrace deck areas at ground and rooftop level

  • 24/7 Manned Security

    Residents benefit from round-the-clock manned security, providing a dependable presence throughout the property. Trained security personnel monitor access and help maintain a safe, controlled environment for residents, visitors, and staff. This continuous security service provides added peace of mind, whether you are at home, returning late, or away from the property.

  • High-Speed Lifts Across Both Towers

    High-speed lifts serve both towers, providing residents and visitors with quick and convenient access between floors. Designed to make everyday movement effortless, the lifts reduce waiting times while supporting smooth circulation throughout the development. Their availability across both towers ensures convenient vertical access for residents, guests, and building staff.

  • Backup Generator for Uninterrupted Power

    A backup generator provides an additional layer of reliability by supporting essential building services during power interruptions. Residents can enjoy greater continuity and convenience without the disruption that can accompany outages. This dependable backup infrastructure is particularly valuable for maintaining essential lighting, security systems, lifts, and other important property services.

  • Borehole Water Supply

    The property benefits from a borehole water supply, providing an additional source of water for the development. This infrastructure helps strengthen the property's water resilience and supports reliable access for residents. Combined with the property's other services, the borehole contributes to a more dependable and convenient residential experience throughout the year.

  • Ample Resident Parking

    Ample resident parking provides convenient space for homeowners and their guests, helping make daily arrivals and departures straightforward. Generous parking provision reduces the need to search for suitable spaces nearby while supporting the practical needs of residents with private vehicles. The dedicated parking facilities complement the property's wider emphasis on comfort and convenience.

  • Staffed Reception/Concierge Area

    A staffed reception and concierge area provides residents with a welcoming point of contact within the property. On-site personnel can assist with general enquiries and help coordinate everyday building needs, adding a polished level of service to the residential experience. The reception area also creates a professional and inviting first impression for residents and their visitors.

Local Amenities

Berkeley sits at the meeting point of two of Nairobi's most in-demand residential pockets — the commercial energy of Westlands and the tree-lined residential calm of Kileleshwa. This dual identity — quiet green suburb by day, connected city address by evening — is what continues to drive strong tenant demand and resale value in this specific pocket of Nairobi.

  • Shopping & Retail

    Enjoy convenient access to Sarit Centre, Westgate Shopping Mall, The Oval, and ABC Place, offering a wide range of retail, dining, and lifestyle amenities.

  • Business Hubs

    Major business destinations are close by, including Westlands' corporate offices, GTC (Global Trade Centre), and the wider CBD, supporting convenient commuting.

  • Schools

    Nearby educational institutions include Peponi House Preparatory School, International School of Kenya (ISK), and Kenton College Preparatory School.

  • Healthcare

    Residents have convenient access to leading medical facilities, including Aga Khan University Hospital and MP Shah Hospital, for comprehensive healthcare services.

  • Green Space

    Nairobi Arboretum and Karura Forest offer nearby opportunities to enjoy nature, outdoor recreation, walking trails, and peaceful green surroundings.

  • Dining & Cafés

    A strong selection of restaurants and coffee houses along the Westlands-Kileleshwa strip provides residents with diverse dining and social options.

  • Hospitality

    Well-known hotels including Villa Rosa Kempinski, Sankara Nairobi, and Ibis Styles Westlands provide nearby accommodation, dining, and hospitality services.

  • Connectivity

    Quick access to the Nairobi Expressway provides a convenient route toward Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and other key destinations across Nairobi.
